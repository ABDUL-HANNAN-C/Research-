# Business Opportunities Positioned to Grow in 2027

*Researched October 2026. Follows on from `automated-ai-business-research.md`.*

## 1. How to spot a market that is about to grow

A market with **high demand and low competition** rarely stays that way for long. Once a market is obviously hot, competitors pile in. The gap shows up in two places: **just before a forcing event**, such as a new law, a platform change or a demographic shift, and in **narrow niches** that big companies ignore. Every pick below is chosen against four tests:

| Test | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Forcing event** | A deadline, law or platform shift that *makes* people act. Demand isn't optional. |
| **Searchable pain** | People are already typing the problem into Google, Reddit or ChatGPT. |
| **Fragmented supply** | Big vendors serve enterprises. Small businesses and individuals are underserved, or stuck with complicated, expensive tools. |
| **Claude-automatable** | The delivery is mostly research, writing, code or document work, so you can run it solo. |

## 3. Top 3 picks in detail

### Pick 1: GEO ("get recommended by ChatGPT") for local and niche businesses
- **Problem:** customers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity "best dentist near me" or "best CRM for plumbers". Most small businesses have no idea whether AI mentions them. Clicks from Google are also falling where AI Overviews appear.
- **Why the window is open:** the big SEO companies serve enterprises. Small business owners are confused and searching for "how to show up in ChatGPT". It is new enough that no one has become the go-to name for a specific niche such as dentists, lawyers, Shopify stores or B2B SaaS.
- **Offer:**
  - Entry offer: an "AI Visibility Audit" ($49–$199). Claude asks the main AI assistants 30 buyer questions and reports where the business shows up versus its competitors.
  - Upsell: monthly fixes ($99–$500/mo) covering FAQ pages, comparison pages, schema markup and consistent business profiles.
  - Later: turn the audit into a self-serve SaaS dashboard.
- **What Claude automates:** running the audit queries, writing the report, drafting fixes and content, and monthly re-checks.
- **Watch out:** don't promise rankings. AI answers change, so sell visibility tracking and improvements, not guarantees. Avoid spammy mass content: AI engines reward specific, corroborated information.

### Pick 2: EU AI Act starter kit for small businesses
- **Problem:** 58% of small businesses now use generative AI, and the AI Act applies to *deployers* (users), not just builders. It also reaches non-EU firms whose AI output is used in the EU. Small businesses don't know what applies to them, and enterprise compliance tools cost thousands.
- **Hot spots:** HR/CV screening, credit and insurance, and education assessment (high-risk). Chatbots (transparency duty).
- **Offer:**
  - A $99–$299 "AI Act Readiness Pack": guided questionnaire → AI-tool inventory → risk classification → AI-use policy, staff guidance, chatbot disclosure text and a vendor checklist.
  - $29/mo to keep it updated as rules change.
- **What Claude automates:** questionnaire logic, document generation, tracking regulatory updates and emailing customers.
- **Watch out:** position it as *documentation and readiness*, not legal advice. Partner with a lawyer for review. Check the current status of EU "digital omnibus" simplification proposals, which may shift some dates.

### Pick 3: E-invoicing for small EU businesses (Germany first)
- **Problem:** German firms with more than €800k turnover must *issue* structured e-invoices (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD) from **1 January 2027**, and all businesses from 2028. Everyone must already be able to *receive* them. Poland's KSeF sanctions start in 2027. Freelancers and small firms are confused about formats and Peppol.
- **Window:** it is a hard deadline, and people are searching for things like "XRechnung erstellen" and "e-Rechnung Pflicht 2027". Many small firms use Excel/Word invoices or older tools.
- **Offer:**
  - A simple web tool that converts PDF/Excel invoices to valid XRechnung/ZUGFeRD.
  - Or a setup service: check current software, configure it and write a 1-page procedure.
  - Content in German targeting the deadline questions.
- **What Claude automates:** building the converter (open standards plus validators), the German-language content and support replies.
- **Watch out:** accounting giants (DATEV, lexoffice, sevDesk) cover this for their own users. Your angle is the **non-users**: freelancers, craftsmen and small exporters selling into Germany. Accuracy matters, so use official validators.

## 4. How to check demand before you build (free, about 1 week)

Have Claude run this checklist on any idea:
1. **Search demand:** Google Trends plus keyword tools (Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest free tier). Look for rising terms with more than 1k monthly searches in your niche and language.
2. **Pain evidence:** Reddit, Quora and Facebook groups. Look for repeated "how do I…" or "is there a tool that…" posts. Collect 20+ real quotes.
3. **Competitor gaps:** read 1–3 star reviews of existing tools on G2, Capterra and app stores. Complaints such as "too expensive", "too complicated" or "not for small businesses" are your gap.
4. **Willingness to pay:** put up a landing page with a price, plus an "early access" button or pre-order. Drive $20–50 of traffic or post in communities. **Aim for 10 signups or 3 pre-sales before building anything.**
5. **Deadline check:** confirm every legal date on the official government page, not blog posts.

## 5. Recommendation

- **If you want the fastest first income:** start with **Pick 1 (GEO audits)**. You can sell it worldwide in English, I can do nearly all the delivery, and it needs no legal expertise.
- **If you want a deadline-driven sprint:** **Pick 3 (German e-invoicing)** gives a clear window from now to January 2027 and on to 2028. It is best if you can sell into Germany or Europe.
- **If you want a durable B2B subscription:** **Pick 2 (AI Act kit)**. It grows as enforcement and procurement checks ramp up through 2027.

A strong combined path: launch GEO audits now (cash flow), then use the income to build one of the compliance tools as a subscription SaaS.

---

## 6. Caveats
- Much of the available data comes from vendor blogs and listicles. Market sizes and statistics are directional, not audited. Legal dates must be verified on official sites (EU Commission, German BMF, Polish Ministry of Finance, French DGFiP).
- "Low competition" is temporary. Speed and picking a narrow niche (one industry, one country, one language) matter more than the category.
- No opportunity is guaranteed to make money.
