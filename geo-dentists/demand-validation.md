# Demand Validation: AI-Visibility Audits for Dental Practices

*October 2026*

## Why dentists (versus lawyers, Shopify stores or SaaS)

| Factor | Dentists | Law firms | Shopify stores | B2B SaaS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence that customers use AI to choose | **Best evidence available.** ~40% of patients reportedly use AI tools to research local health services; 45% of consumers use AI for local recommendations (up from 6% a year earlier) | Little data on how clients find firms | Agentic shopping still early | Strong, but buyers are sophisticated |
| Value of one new customer | High: a patient is worth $1,000s over time, and implants or Invisalign alone cost $3k–$6k | Very high | Low to medium | High |
| Number of businesses | ~200k US practices, and many more worldwide | Many | Many | Fewer |
| Already spending on marketing | Yes, most pay SEO or marketing agencies | Yes | Some | Yes |
| Easy to reach | Public listings, emails on websites, Facebook groups | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Competition for done-for-you AI visibility | **Low.** A May 2026 dental AI visibility study exists, but few productized offers | Medium | Medium | High (many GEO tools target SaaS) |
| Regulatory risk of the service | Low (marketing, not clinical) | Higher (legal advertising rules) | Low | Low |

**Finding:** dentists combine the clearest "patients ask AI" evidence with a high value per customer and little done-for-you competition.

## Key evidence

- **Patients use AI to find care.** Delmain's 2026 compilation: 40% of patients use AI tools to research local health services, mostly in ChatGPT. The original study isn't named, so treat it as directional.
- **AI relies on directories.** The 5W "Dental AI Visibility Index 2026" found that AI answers to dental queries draw mostly on about nine directory and review sites (Google Business Profile, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp…). Individual practices rarely appear on their own merits. **This is the gap we sell into:** fixable, concrete, and invisible to the practice owner.
- **Traffic pressure.** Reports cite click-through drops of around 30%+ when Google AI Overviews appear, so practices feel the decline without knowing why.

| Self-serve GEO dashboards | Otterly (~$29+/mo), Peec AI (~$95+/mo), Profound ($99–$499+/mo), Semrush add-on (~$99/mo) | $29–$500+/mo | Built for marketers. A dentist won't set up prompt tracking. Most only report and don't fix anything |

| PR firms (e.g. 5W) | Enterprise and DSO focused | High | Not aimed at single practices |

**Positioning:** "Done-for-you, dentist-specific, plain English, priced below an agency retainer." The free 3-question check is the hook, since seeing a competitor named instead of you is a strong, visual reason to buy.

## Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI answers vary from run to run | Sell a snapshot plus trend, never guarantees. Re-run questions and report ranges |
| Agencies add this service | Move fast, specialise in dental, and build a monthly-tracking subscription |
| Dentists are busy and hard to reach | Lead with a free personalised result showing their own name, not a generic pitch |
| Overselling | Clear FAQ: no guarantee. A human reviews every report |

## Validation plan (2 weeks, about $0–$50)

1. **Run 10 free checks**, choosing practices in one city. Use `audit-tool/audit.py --limit 3`.
2. **Email each practice** its personalised result: "We asked ChatGPT 'best emergency dentist in [city]'. It recommended X, Y, Z, but not you. Here's the full answer." Link to the landing page for the full audit.
3. **Post in dental owner communities** (Facebook groups, r/Dentistry, dental office manager groups), offering free checks.
4. **Success criteria:** ≥20% reply rate on outreach, and **at least 3 paid audits ($149, or $75 founding price) within 2 weeks**. If you hit that, continue to the monthly plan. If not, test a different niche (law firms, med spas, vets) with the same tool by swapping `questions.txt`.
