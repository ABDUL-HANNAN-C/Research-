### Q2: I need an emergency dentist in Charlotte, NC today, who should I call?

- **Smile Carolina**: Offices in Charlotte and Concord. They keep time open each day for toothaches, broken teeth and lost fillings.
- **Smile Charlotte**: Offers same-day emergency appointments.
- **Holt Family Dentistry**: Same-day emergency care with Dr. Gordon-Holt and Dr. Fusco.

**Tips:**
- When you call, ask whether they can see you today, whether they take new patients and your insurance, and roughly what an emergency exam costs.
- **Go to the ER or call 911 instead** if your face is swelling up, the swelling is spreading to your eye or neck, you have trouble breathing or swallowing, you have a fever with tooth pain, or you have bleeding that won't stop or a serious injury to your jaw or face.
- **If a tooth gets knocked out**, hold it by the crown (the part you chew with), not the root. Keep it in milk and get to a dentist within about an hour.

### Q3: Which dentists in Charlotte, NC are good with nervous or anxious patients?

Here are several Charlotte practices that say on their own websites that they cater to nervous or anxious patients. I found them through a web search, so this comes from the practices' own marketing, not from independent reviews or my endorsement.

- **Advanced Dentistry of Charlotte** (Dr. Chris Bowman): Offers oral and conscious sedation. Dr. Bowman is a member of the Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation. It serves Charlotte, Myers Park and Dilworth.
- **Charlotte Dentistry**: Has a page about easing dental anxiety. It offers laughing gas, which wears off quickly so you can drive yourself home, and oral sedation (a pill) for stronger anxiety or longer procedures. It also mentions comforts like blankets.
- **Charlotte Midtown Dentistry**: Offers sedation dentistry for comfort during procedures.
- **Layton Dentistry**: Near Uptown. Offers laughing gas, oral sedation and IV sedation, which is useful if you have severe anxiety.
- **Holt Family Dentistry**: Offers sedation for patients who feel nervous in the chair or are bothered by the sound of dental equipment.
- **Dentistry of the Queen City**: Offers three types of sedation and talks through the options with you before treatment.
- **Blakeney Smiles**: Ballantyne area. Uses sedation to help patients who've had bad experiences in the past, are sensitive to discomfort, or struggle with long appointments.
- **Dental Wellness of Charlotte**: South Charlotte. Offers gentle sedation options.

**Tips for choosing:**
1. Check recent Google reviews and search for words like "anxiety," "nervous" or "gentle."
2. Ask for a consultation first, with no treatment, just to meet the dentist.
3. Ask which kinds of sedation they offer. IV sedation usually needs extra certification, and you'll need someone to drive you home after oral or IV sedation.
4. Check that they take your insurance. Sedation often costs extra.
5. Tell them about your anxiety when you book. Good practices will offer things like breaks, stop signals and explaining each step.
