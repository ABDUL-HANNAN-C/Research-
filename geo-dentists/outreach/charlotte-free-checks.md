# Charlotte, NC: Free 3-Question Checks (Preliminary)

*Run 9 October 2026 by Claude using live web search, standing in for "what an AI assistant tells a patient".*

> **Important:** this is a preliminary manual check, not a run of `audit.py`. There's no API key yet, so I asked the 3 questions myself through my own web-search tool. Results from ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can differ, and AI answers change from day to day. **Before quoting a result to a practice, re-check it yourself:** ask ChatGPT the same question and screenshot the answer, or run `audit.py` once you have a key. Never send a claim you haven't seen with your own eyes.

## The 3 questions and who the AI named

The questions are city-wide, so the answers apply to every practice in Charlotte.

| # | Patient question | Practices the AI named | Sources the AI leaned on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Who is the best dentist in Charlotte, NC?" | Pearl. Dentistry, Skyview Dentistry, Sturner Dentistry | Charlotte Magazine Top Dentists, WSOC-TV "best dentists" (Yelp-based), Lantern rankings |
| 2 | "I need an emergency dentist in Charlotte, NC today, who should I call?" | Aspen Dental (3 locations, chain), Charlotte Dentistry, Haydn G. Jones II DDS | Aspen Dental location pages, Charlotte Dentistry ads, a 24/7 emergency directory |
| 3 | "Which dentists in Charlotte, NC are good with nervous or anxious patients?" | Layton Dentistry, Providence Oral Surgery, LandMark / Mint Hill Dentistry, Charlotte Center for Cosmetic Dentistry | Lantern profiles, practice sedation pages |

### What this shows
- **Only about 10 practices get named** across all three answers, in a metro with hundreds of dentists.
- **Directories decide the answer:** Lantern, Charlotte Magazine and WSOC's Yelp-based list come up again and again. Being missing or thin on those sites means being missing from AI answers.
- **The emergency question goes to a chain.** Aspen Dental wins it because each location has its own "emergency dental services" page. An independent practice with a clear emergency page and same-day hours could compete.
- Some low-quality and spam pages were cited too, so the AI's sources are not always good ones. That is part of the pitch.
