# Outreach Emails: Charlotte Dental Practices

## Before you send (important)

1. **Verify the claim yourself.** Ask ChatGPT the exact question and screenshot the answer. Attach that screenshot, not my table. If the practice *is* named when you check, don't email them that claim.
2. **Send from your own real email address**, ideally on your own domain (e.g. you@askedfirst.com), not a bulk tool. Send 10 emails by hand, not 1,000.
3. **US anti-spam law (CAN-SPAM)** requires your real name, a **physical mailing address** (a PO box or virtual address is fine) and a clear way to opt out. Honour opt-outs immediately. All templates below include these.
4. Address the **office manager or practice owner** by name if the website lists one.
5. **Don't** promise rankings, imply you're from Google or OpenAI, or invent statistics.
