Questions

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. AI answers change and vary between assistants. What we do is measure where you stand, fix what's within your control, and track the trend month by month.

Is this the same as SEO?

It builds on SEO. AI assistants lean heavily on directories, review sites and clear, specific pages. Many practices that rank well on Google are still missing from AI answers.

Do you need access to our website or accounts?

Not for the audit. For the ongoing plan, we can either send you the content to publish or work with your web person.

How is the audit done?

We use an AI assistant with live web search to ask real patient questions, then a person reviews every report before it reaches you.
